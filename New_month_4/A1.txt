MANIFESTO FOR DINING HALL PREFECT – 2025/2026 ACADEMIC YEAR

Madam Headteacher, Honourable Teachers, Fellow Students, Ladies and Gentlemen.

I greet you all in the spirit of humility and service. My name is Adwoa Serwaa, and I stand before you to declare my candidacy for the post of Dining Hall Prefect.

The dining hall, dear school community, is the heartbeat of our daily life. It is where we break bread—or rather, where we dip our banku, eat our waakye, and swallow our fufu. It is where friendships are cemented and energy is restored. Yet, I have observed with a heavy heart the challenges that plague this sacred space: long, disorderly queues where the strong push out the weak; tables left greasy and uninviting; leftover rice and stew thrown carelessly into bins while some students still go home hungry; and even occasional disrespect toward kitchen mothers who toil before dawn to feed us.

Fellow students, this is not the legacy we want.

If given the mandate by your kind votes, I pledge the following:

One: I will introduce a clear lane system for each food type—banku, rice, soup, and drinking water. No cutting, no cheating. Two prefects on duty will enforce this fairly.

Two: I will form a rotating “Clean Table Squad” to wipe and arrange tables after every sitting. By the time the bell rings for the next batch, every surface will be spotless.

Three: I will work with the catering staff to implement a “Share-Not-Waste” basket. Leftover food in good condition will be offered to students who need extra, with proper supervision. No more bins full of kenkey.

Four: I will place a suggestion box at the entrance. Every complaint—cold soup, missing spoon, rude behaviour—will be answered within twenty-four hours.

Ladies and gentlemen, I will lead not with pride but with patience. I will be the first to arrive and the last to leave. I will listen to every student, from the JHS one to the senior final year.

Therefore, I humbly ask for your vote. Vote for Adwoa Serwaa. Let us restore dignity to our dining hall.

God bless our school, and God bless you all.
