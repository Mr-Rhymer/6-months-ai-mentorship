Adwoa Serwaa is running for Dining Hall Prefect and aims to address the challenges facing the school's dining hall, including disorderly queues, unclean tables, food waste, and disrespect towards staff. If elected, she pledges to introduce a lane system, create a "Clean Table Squad", implement a "Share-Not-Waste" basket, and establish a suggestion box to improve the dining experience. Adwoa promises to lead with patience and humility, listening to every student and working to restore dignity to the dining hall.
